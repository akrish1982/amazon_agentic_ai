COMMERCIAL & INDUSTRIAL (C&I) LENDING POLICY
Version 2.8 | Effective: April 1, 2025 | Risk Tier: High
Regulation References: BSA, ECOA
Applicable Loan Types: C&I

1. OVERVIEW
Commercial & Industrial (C&I) loans finance business operations, equipment, working capital,
and asset-based lending. Unlike CRE, C&I loans are primarily supported by business cash flow
rather than real estate collateral.

2. ELIGIBLE LOAN PURPOSES
- Working capital (revolving lines of credit, seasonal facilities)
- Equipment and machinery purchase
- Business acquisition financing
- Inventory financing
- Accounts receivable financing (factoring arrangements)
- Leasehold improvements
- Supply chain / trade finance

3. FINANCIAL ANALYSIS

3.1 Cash Flow Analysis
Primary repayment source is business operating cash flow:
- Analyze trailing 3-year EBITDA trend (must be positive and growing or stable)
- Calculate DSCR: EBITDA / Total Debt Service ≥ 1.25x
- Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio: (EBITDA - CapEx - Taxes) / Debt Service ≥ 1.10x
- Stress test: DSCR at 10% revenue decline must remain ≥ 1.0x

3.2 Working Capital Analysis
- Current ratio: minimum 1.20x
- Quick ratio: minimum 0.80x
- Accounts receivable days: benchmark to industry median (NAICS-specific)
- Inventory days: benchmark to industry median
- Accounts payable days: ensure no stretching indicating liquidity stress

3.3 Leverage Analysis
- Debt/EBITDA: maximum 4.0x for standard credits, 5.0x for sponsor-backed
- Senior Debt/EBITDA: maximum 3.0x
- Total Liabilities/Tangible Net Worth: maximum 4.0x

4. COLLATERAL REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Collateral for Lines of Credit (Revolving)
- Asset-based: 80% eligible AR (<90 days) + 50% finished goods inventory
- Cash flow-based (unsecured): available for investment-grade or established credits
- Borrowing base certificate required monthly (quarterly for credits <$500K)

4.2 Collateral for Term Loans
- Equipment: 70-80% of appraised forced liquidation value
- Blanket business lien (UCC-1 filing on all assets)
- Personal guarantee of all owners ≥20%

4.3 Lien Priority
- Bank must hold first-priority perfected security interest
- UCC search required prior to commitment; lien search in all states of operation
- Re-search at closing; re-lien if existing liens found

5. INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC GUIDELINES

5.1 Technology Companies
- Revenue recognition: verify GAAP compliance for SaaS/subscription models
- Key person risk: assess dependency on founders/key employees
- Intellectual property: confirm no material IP litigation

5.2 Healthcare/Medical
- Accounts receivable: distinguish Medicare/Medicaid (collectibility haircut 20%)
- Regulatory risk: check CMS provider enrollment status
- Malpractice insurance: confirm adequate coverage

5.3 Retail / Consumer
- Seasonality adjustment: normalize for seasonal revenue peaks
- E-commerce competition risk: evaluate in credit memo
- Lease obligations: include in fixed charge calculation

6. PRICING AND STRUCTURE

6.1 Interest Rate Pricing
Pricing must reflect risk-adjusted return:
- Tier 1 (DSCR >1.50x, score >720): SOFR + 175–225 bps
- Tier 2 (DSCR 1.25-1.50x, score 680-720): SOFR + 225–300 bps
- Tier 3 (DSCR 1.10-1.25x, score 650-679): SOFR + 300–400 bps

6.2 Covenant Requirements
For loans >$1M, include financial covenants:
- Minimum DSCR tested annually (typically 1.20x)
- Maximum leverage ratio tested quarterly
- Minimum liquidity covenant (current ratio or minimum cash balance)
- Material adverse change clause
