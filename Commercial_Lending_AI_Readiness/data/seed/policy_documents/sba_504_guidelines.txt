SBA 504 LOAN PROGRAM GUIDELINES
Version 2.4 | Effective: October 1, 2023 | Risk Tier: High
Regulation References: SBA, ECOA, TILA
Applicable Loan Types: SBA

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW
The SBA 504 Loan Program provides long-term, fixed-rate financing for major fixed assets
through Certified Development Companies (CDCs). The program is authorized under
Title V of the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, 15 U.S.C. § 695 et seq.

2. ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Borrower Eligibility
Business must:
- Be a for-profit business operating in the United States
- Have tangible net worth not exceeding $20 million
- Have average net income not exceeding $6.5 million after federal taxes for prior 2 years
- Not be in the business of passive real estate investment
- Not be a financial business (banks, lending firms, insurance companies)

2.2 Use of Proceeds (Eligible)
504 proceeds may only be used for fixed assets:
- Purchase of existing buildings
- Construction of new facilities
- Renovation or modernization of existing facilities
- Purchase of long-term machinery and equipment (10+ year useful life)
- Improvement of land and utilities

2.3 Use of Proceeds (Ineligible)
- Working capital or inventory
- Refinancing existing debt (with limited exceptions under SBA SOP 50 10 7)
- Debt repayment
- Equity investment
- Real estate held for investment (passive income)

3. LOAN STRUCTURE

3.1 Standard 504 Structure
- Bank (First Mortgage): 50% of total project cost — conventional terms, bank sets rate
- CDC/SBA (Second Mortgage — 504 Debenture): 40% of total project cost — 10 or 20 year term, fixed rate
- Borrower Equity: minimum 10% (15% for start-ups <2 years; 20% for special-use properties)

3.2 Maximum Debenture Amounts
- Standard project: $5,000,000
- Energy public policy goals: $5,500,000 (renewable energy or 10% energy reduction)
- Manufacturing: $5,500,000

3.3 Job Creation/Retention Requirement
General rule: create or retain 1 job per $90,000 of SBA debenture ($140,000 for manufacturing).
Alternative: meet a community development or public policy goal.

4. UNDERWRITING STANDARDS

4.1 Debt Service Coverage
Minimum DSCR of 1.10x on project cash flow (Year 2 stabilized).
For acquisitions: use historical NOI with 10% vacancy haircut.
For new construction: independent market study required.

4.2 Collateral
First mortgage on financed property is required.
SBA takes second position behind bank's first mortgage.
Additional collateral may be required based on LTV and DSCR.

4.3 Personal Guarantee
All owners with 20%+ ownership must personally guarantee.
Spouses of principals must sign guarantee if state law requires.

5. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- Completed SBA Form 1244 (Application for Section 504 Loans)
- Business and personal tax returns (3 years)
- Personal financial statements (SBA Form 413)
- Business plan with 3-year projections (for start-ups)
- Environmental questionnaire (Phase I ESA may be required)
- Appraisal by SBA-approved appraiser
- Flood zone determination certificate

6. PROCESSING TIMELINE
- CDC submits package to SBA: within 5 business days of complete application
- SBA approval: typically 5-10 business days
- Debenture funding: occurs quarterly (SBA pools debentures)
- Total timeline: 60-90 days from application to closing
