KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) AND ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) POLICY
Version 7.1 | Effective: March 15, 2025 | Risk Tier: Critical
Regulation References: BSA, AML, OFAC
Applicable Loan Types: SBA, CRE, C&I, construction

1. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION PROGRAM (CIP)

1.1 Required Identifying Information
For all new commercial borrowers, collect and verify:
- Legal entity name (exact match to Secretary of State registration)
- Principal place of business address (no P.O. Box)
- EIN / TIN (IRS Form W-9 or equivalent)
- Formation documents: Articles of Incorporation/Organization, Operating Agreement
- List of all beneficial owners (25% threshold per FinCEN CDD Rule)

1.2 Beneficial Ownership Requirements
Per 31 C.F.R. § 1010.230 (FinCEN CDD Rule, effective May 11, 2018):
- Collect name, DOB, address, and SSN/passport for each beneficial owner
- Beneficial owner = individual who owns 25% or more equity interest
- Control prong: one individual with significant managerial control must always be identified
- Certification form must be signed by a legal entity representative

2. RISK-BASED CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE

2.1 Customer Risk Scoring Model
Each customer receives a composite risk score (0-100) based on:
- Industry risk (NAICS-based: high-risk industries score 40+ points)
- Geographic risk (FATF high-risk jurisdictions, domestic high-intensity areas)
- Transaction pattern risk (cash-intensive, structuring indicators)
- PEP/Sanctions status (Politically Exposed Person, SDN list match)

2.2 Risk Tiers and Review Frequency
- Low Risk (0-30): Annual review, standard monitoring
- Medium Risk (31-65): Semi-annual review, enhanced monitoring
- High Risk (66-100): Quarterly review, Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) required

2.3 High-Risk Industry Categories (partial list)
The following NAICS codes trigger automatic enhanced due diligence:
- 522390: Money Services Businesses
- 713210: Casinos and gaming
- 5912: Pharmacies (potential diversion risk)
- 111998: Cannabis-adjacent agriculture
- 522110: Banks in FATF non-compliant jurisdictions

3. SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY REPORTING

3.1 SAR Filing Thresholds
File FinCEN SAR (Form 111) when:
- Transaction ≥$5,000 and bank knows, suspects, or has reason to suspect:
  a. Funds from illegal activity
  b. Transaction designed to evade BSA requirements (structuring)
  c. No lawful purpose or unusual nature
- $25,000 threshold for insider involvement

3.2 SAR Filing Timeline
- Identify: within 30 calendar days of detection
- File: within 30 days of identification (60 days if suspect unknown)
- SAR retention: 5 years from date of filing
- Do not tip off subject of SAR (tipping-off prohibition)

4. OFAC SANCTIONS COMPLIANCE

4.1 SDN Screening Requirements
Screen against OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list:
- At account/loan origination
- When customer information changes
- When OFAC updates the SDN list (receive automated alerts)
- Periodic rescreening: at least annually for all active accounts

4.2 Match Resolution
- Potential match: escalate to BSA Officer within 1 business day
- Confirmed match: block transaction, file OFAC report, do not release without OFAC license
- Document all screening results in customer file

5. CURRENCY TRANSACTION REPORTING

5.1 CTR Filing Requirements (31 C.F.R. § 1010.311)
File FinCEN CTR (Form 112) for:
- Cash transactions >$10,000 in a single day
- Multiple transactions by or for same person aggregating >$10,000

5.2 CTR Exemptions
Eligible for Phase II exemption after 12 months of established account history:
- Non-listed businesses with predictable cash patterns
- File FinCEN 110 (Designation of Exempt Person)
- Re-examine exemption annually
