AI AND DATA GOVERNANCE POLICY FOR LENDING SYSTEMS
Version 1.0 | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Risk Tier: High
Regulation References: ECOA, BSA
Applicable Loan Types: SBA, CRE, C&I, construction

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes governance standards for AI systems and data used in commercial lending,
ensuring compliance with fair lending laws, model risk management guidance (SR 11-7), and
emerging AI regulatory expectations.

2. DATA QUALITY STANDARDS FOR AI CONSUMPTION

2.1 Completeness Requirements
All data fed to AI systems must meet minimum completeness thresholds:
- CRM records: ≥95% completeness for mandatory fields (EIN, contact, KYC status)
- Financial data: ≥90% completeness for current-year financials
- Compliance data: 100% completeness for OFAC screening dates

2.2 Freshness Standards
- Customer risk profiles: refreshed within 90 days
- Credit bureau pulls: within 120 days of loan decision
- Beneficial ownership certifications: within 3 years or on material change
- Property appraisals: within 12 months

2.3 Data Lineage Requirements
All data used in AI-assisted decisions must have documented lineage:
- Source system identification (CRM, LOS, core banking)
- Transformation history (ETL pipeline steps)
- Quality check results and exception handling
- Timestamp of last validation

3. MODEL RISK MANAGEMENT (SR 11-7 COMPLIANCE)

3.1 Model Validation Requirements
AI models used in credit decisioning require:
- Independent model validation before production deployment
- Annual ongoing validation
- Performance monitoring: Gini coefficient, PSI (Population Stability Index), Kolmogorov-Smirnov
- Outcome testing: adverse action accuracy, disparate impact analysis

3.2 Fair Lending AI Testing
Before deploying AI in credit decisions:
- Disparate impact analysis: test for statistical significance across protected classes
- Proxy variable analysis: identify and remove variables correlated with prohibited bases
- Model explainability: must be able to provide specific adverse action reasons

4. KNOWLEDGE BASE GOVERNANCE

4.1 Policy Document Management
- All policy documents must be version-controlled with effective dates
- Superseded versions must be archived (not deleted) with clear deprecation dates
- AI retrieval systems must use effective-date filtering to prevent retrieval of expired policy

4.2 Metadata Requirements for AI Retrieval
Every document ingested into the AI knowledge base must have:
- Document type classification
- Applicable regulation references
- Applicable loan type tags
- Risk tier classification
- Effective and expiry dates
- Jurisdiction (federal vs. state-specific)
- Version number

5. AGENT AND AUTOMATION GOVERNANCE

5.1 Human-in-the-Loop Requirements
AI agents may assist but not make final credit decisions. Required human review for:
- All loan approvals (AI recommendation must be reviewed by licensed officer)
- Adverse action: human must review and sign off on reasons cited
- OFAC/sanctions matches: always requires human decision

5.2 Audit Trail
All AI agent actions must be logged:
- Query inputs and tool calls
- Retrieved context sources (with document IDs)
- Recommendations made
- Human review outcome
Retention: 7 years for credit-related AI logs
