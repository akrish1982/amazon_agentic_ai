COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LENDING POLICY
Version 5.0 | Effective: July 1, 2025 | Risk Tier: High
Regulation References: ECOA, CRA, BSA
Applicable Loan Types: CRE

1. SCOPE
This policy governs all commercial real estate (CRE) loan originations, including
income-producing properties, owner-occupied commercial real estate (OOCRE),
construction loans, and CRE loan participations.

2. PROPERTY TYPES AND RISK CLASSIFICATION

2.1 Standard Risk Properties
- Multi-family residential (5+ units)
- Retail strip centers (established tenants, <70% single tenant)
- Office buildings (suburban, multi-tenant)
- Industrial/warehouse
- Owner-occupied commercial (business operations >51% of space)

2.2 Elevated Risk Properties (enhanced underwriting required)
- Hotels and hospitality (cyclical revenue, management dependency)
- Assisted living and healthcare facilities (regulatory risk)
- Single-tenant properties (lease rollover risk)
- Retail: mall anchors and big-box (secular headwinds)
- Land loans (no income stream, speculative)

2.3 Restricted Properties (require senior credit approval)
- Cannabis-related properties
- Adult entertainment
- Properties with known environmental contamination
- Churches and non-profit real estate

3. UNDERWRITING CRITERIA

3.1 Income Analysis
- Use trailing 12-month actual NOI for stabilized properties
- Apply 5% vacancy factor for fully occupied properties
- Management fee: minimum 5% of EGI even if self-managed
- Capital reserve: minimum $0.15/SF/year for office/retail, $0.10/SF for industrial

3.2 Market Analysis Required For:
- Loans >$1M: local market rent comparables
- Loans >$3M: third-party market study
- Construction loans: pre-leasing requirements (typically 30-50% depending on market)

3.3 Borrower Analysis
- Minimum 3 years of profitable CRE ownership experience for investment properties
- Net worth ≥ loan amount (including guarantors)
- Liquidity ≥ 12 months of debt service post-closing
- Global DSCR ≥ 1.20x (all existing obligations + proposed debt)

4. CRE CONCENTRATION LIMITS
Per regulatory guidance (2006 CRE Guidance, FDIC):
- Construction and land development: ≤100% of risk-based capital
- Total CRE (non-owner-occupied): ≤300% of risk-based capital

Loans approaching concentration limits require:
- Enhanced stress testing (200bps rate shock)
- Board-approved concentration risk management plan
- Quarterly reporting to senior management

5. ENVIRONMENTAL REVIEW
- Phase I Environmental Site Assessment required for all loans >$500,000
- Phase II required if Phase I identifies Recognized Environmental Conditions (RECs)
- ASTM E1527-21 standard applies
- Environmental indemnification agreement required in all cases

6. APPRAISAL STANDARDS
Per FIRREA and USPAP:
- State-certified general appraiser for all CRE appraisals
- Independence requirement: appraiser cannot be compensated based on loan outcome
- Review appraisal required for loans >$5,000,000
- Desk review acceptable only for renewals with no material change

7. LOAN TERMS

7.1 Amortization Schedules
- Multi-family: up to 30 years amortization, 10-year term
- Office/retail/industrial: up to 25 years amortization, 5-10 year term
- Hotels: up to 20 years amortization, 5-7 year term
- Construction: interest-only during construction, convert to permanent

7.2 Rate Structures
- Fixed rate: typically 3, 5, or 7 year fixed, then floating
- Floating rate: SOFR + spread (LIBOR legacy loans: complete transition by June 2023)
- Rate floors: minimum 4.0% on all CRE loans
