EQUAL CREDIT OPPORTUNITY ACT (ECOA) FAIR LENDING POLICY
Version 4.1 | Effective: January 1, 2024 | Risk Tier: Critical
Regulation References: ECOA, TILA
Applicable Loan Types: SBA, CRE, C&I, construction

1. POLICY STATEMENT
The Bank is committed to fair lending in all credit decisions, consistent with the Equal
Credit Opportunity Act (15 U.S.C. § 1691 et seq.) and Regulation B (12 C.F.R. Part 202).
No applicant shall be discriminated against on any prohibited basis.

2. PROHIBITED BASES (ECOA/Regulation B)
- Race or color
- Religion
- National origin
- Sex or gender identity (including sexual orientation per CFPB guidance)
- Marital status
- Age (provided applicant has capacity to contract)
- Receipt of income from public assistance programs
- Exercise of rights under the Consumer Credit Protection Act

3. PERMISSIBLE CREDIT FACTORS
Credit decisions must be based solely on:
- Credit history and score (business and personal)
- Debt-to-income or DSCR ratios
- Collateral quality and coverage
- Business cash flow and profitability
- Industry and market risk
- Management experience and capacity

4. ADVERSE ACTION REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Notice Timing
- Consumer credit: within 30 days of application (Reg B §202.9)
- Business credit: within a reasonable time (30 days recommended)
- Incomplete application: notice within 30 days with information needed to complete

4.2 Required Notice Content
Written adverse action notice must state:
- Action taken (denial, counteroffer, etc.)
- Name and address of creditor
- ECOA statement of rights
- Name and address of federal regulatory agency
- Specific reasons for adverse action (or right to request them within 60 days)

4.3 Specific Reasons Requirement
Vague reasons are not acceptable. Use specific factual reasons:
ACCEPTABLE: "Credit score of 620 is below our minimum of 650"
NOT ACCEPTABLE: "Credit history" or "insufficient creditworthiness"

4.4 Record Retention
- Business credit applications: 25 months from date of action
- Consumer credit applications: 25 months from date of action
- All records sufficient to evidence compliance

5. SPECIAL RULE FOR BUSINESS CREDIT
For business credit with gross revenues >$1 million in preceding fiscal year:
- Bank may, but is not required to, provide specific reasons
- Must maintain records of the credit decision
- Adverse action notice timeline applies

6. FAIR LENDING TESTING AND MONITORING

6.1 File Review
- Quarterly random file review: 5% of approved and denied applications
- Annual comparative file review: compare similarly situated applicants across protected classes
- Pricing analysis: test for unexplained pricing disparities

6.2 HMDA/CRA Data Integrity
For residential mortgage components within commercial loans:
- Ensure accurate HMDA data collection and reporting (Reg C)
- Geocode all loan applications to census tract level
- Annual HMDA LAR submission by March 1

7. TRAINING
All loan officers, underwriters, and credit staff must complete:
- Annual ECOA/Fair Lending training (minimum 2 hours)
- New hire training within 30 days
- Enhanced training for staff with fair lending findings

8. COMPLAINTS AND MONITORING
- Centralized tracking of all fair lending complaints
- Monthly report to Chief Compliance Officer
- Annual fair lending risk assessment presented to Board
- Regulatory examination preparation: maintain complete adverse action files
