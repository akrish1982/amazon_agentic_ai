COMMERCIAL LENDING UNDERWRITING POLICY
Version 3.2 | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Risk Tier: Critical
Regulation References: ECOA, BSA, CRA, TILA
Applicable Loan Types: CRE, C&I, SBA, construction

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy establishes underwriting standards for all commercial loan originations exceeding $250,000.
It applies to all relationship managers, credit analysts, and senior underwriters.

2. CREDIT QUALITY STANDARDS

2.1 Minimum Credit Score Requirements
- C&I loans <$1M: minimum business credit score 650, owner personal FICO 680
- C&I loans $1M–$5M: minimum business credit score 680, owner personal FICO 700
- CRE loans all amounts: minimum business credit score 660, owner personal FICO 700
- SBA loans: follow SBA SOP 50 10 7 standards; minimum FICO 650

2.2 Financial Statement Requirements
Borrowers must provide:
- 3 years of business tax returns (IRS Form 1120 or 1065)
- 2 years of personal tax returns for all guarantors with 20%+ ownership
- CPA-prepared or audited financial statements for loans >$2M
- Interim financials within 90 days of application date

3. DEBT SERVICE COVERAGE RATIO (DSCR) REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Minimum DSCR Thresholds by Loan Type
DSCR = Net Operating Income (NOI) / Annual Debt Service (ADS)

- C&I loans: minimum DSCR 1.25x (global cash flow analysis required)
- CRE loans: minimum DSCR 1.20x (property-level NOI used)
- SBA 7(a): minimum DSCR 1.15x (SBA requirement, use historical average)
- SBA 504: minimum DSCR 1.10x on project cash flow
- Construction loans: minimum DSCR 1.20x on stabilized pro forma

3.2 DSCR Calculation Standards
- Use 3-year average NOI for existing businesses
- For new projects, use Year-2 stabilized projections with 10% vacancy factor
- All existing debt obligations must be included in ADS calculation
- Seasonal businesses: use 12-month average, not peak period

4. LOAN-TO-VALUE (LTV) STANDARDS

4.1 Maximum LTV by Collateral Type
- Owner-occupied commercial real estate: 80% LTV
- Investment/income-producing CRE: 75% LTV
- Equipment and machinery: 70% of appraised FMV
- Accounts receivable: 70% of eligible AR (aged <90 days)
- Inventory: 50% of finished goods value
- SBA 504 total project financing: 90% (SBA debenture 40% + bank 50% + equity 10%)

4.2 Appraisal Requirements
- Licensed MAI appraiser required for CRE collateral >$500,000
- Two appraisals required for CRE collateral >$2,000,000
- Desktop appraisals not acceptable for primary collateral
- Appraisals must be dated within 12 months of closing

5. COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

5.1 ECOA Compliance (Equal Credit Opportunity Act)
All credit decisions must be based solely on creditworthiness factors. Prohibited bases include
race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, or receipt of public assistance.
Adverse action notices must be issued within 30 days per Regulation B §202.9.

5.2 BSA/AML Requirements
- CIP (Customer Identification Program) verification before account opening
- Beneficial ownership certification required for all legal entity borrowers
- Enhanced due diligence for high-risk industries: MSBs, cannabis-adjacent, cash-intensive
- Transaction monitoring alerts must be reviewed within 5 business days

5.3 CRA Considerations
CRE loans in the bank's assessment area are eligible for CRA credit.
LMI borrowers and projects in LMI census tracts receive enhanced CRA consideration.

6. APPROVAL AUTHORITY

6.1 Delegated Lending Authority
- Relationship Manager: up to $250,000
- Senior RM / Team Lead: up to $750,000
- Branch Credit Officer: up to $2,000,000
- Regional Credit Officer: up to $5,000,000
- Chief Credit Officer: up to $15,000,000
- Credit Committee: above $15,000,000

6.2 Mandatory Escalation Triggers
Regardless of loan amount, escalate to next level for:
- Borrower with AML flag or OFAC watch list match
- DSCR below minimum threshold
- Exceptions to policy (document all exceptions)
- Existing classified or criticized credits
